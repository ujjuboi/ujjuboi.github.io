# Study Plan: [Subject Title]

Study days are **dated calendar days**, not numbered weeks. Nodes run on
consecutive calendar days in a **3 study + 1 rest rhythm**: three study days,
then one rest day, repeating (Day 1 — Day 2 — Day 3 — Rest Day — Day 4 — ...).
Every 4th calendar day is a rest day. A content week spans roughly Day 1-3 (+
rest), Day 4-6 (+ rest), etc. Day numbers continue across phase boundaries
(they never restart at a new phase).

Day headings:

- Study: `### Day 1: Sun, Sep 20 — [Day Topic]`
- Rest: `### Rest Day: Wed, Sep 23 — Review & Recovery`
